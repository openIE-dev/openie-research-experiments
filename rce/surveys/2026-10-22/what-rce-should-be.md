# What RCE should be

The Reality Code Engine should be a finite-control, content-addressed, partial-order-logged state-machine language whose only proved object is also its shipped executor, and it should stop claiming anything the computation cannot deliver. Across seven areas the state of the art converges on the same verdict: RCE's proved invariants (total deterministic δ, name-free hashing, fold law, refusal leaves state unchanged) are exactly the preconditions the classical theory needs, and the obstacles are all computational, not conceptual. The minimal Mealy machine is a unique canonical form only for the control component with data abstracted to guard predicates ([D'Antoni–Veanes, POPL 2014](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/sfaMinimizationPOPL14.pdf)); the IR should be a Merkle DAG of α-erased, normalised bodies keyed by canonical-JSON digest, with the Unison/Dhall lesson that names and spelling live in an unhashed complement ([Unison](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/), [Dhall](https://github.com/dhall-lang/dhall-lang/blob/master/standard/binary.md)); the log should keep the admitter's canonical total order for admission but hash entries by their causal deps so the digest is linearization-invariant, with state hashes only at cuts; synthesis should close holes by type-pruned, equivalence-collapsed enumeration (ECTA: 88% vs 64% on the Hoogle+ suites, [ICFP 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.07828)) and the gate must measure spec completeness, because a verifier alone accepts `ensures true` ([SpecRL, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05820)); the cost model splits into target-blind C.6 budget units that stay in D and a per-target calibrated coefficient vector `E[target]` logged as `host.Calibrated`, with a time-dominated regime for big cores where power was **189.8 ± 0.5 W across every implementation** ([van Kempen et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05460v4)); the 2026 agent-language field is 42 entries, 36 of them listed this year, and RCE's only defensible differentiator is that replay and injectivity are Lean theorems rather than runtime promises ([agentlanguages.dev](https://agentlanguages.dev)); and the proof discipline should follow SpecTec and CakeML (generate prose, checker tables and Lean from one rule source; run the proved core as the executor of record) rather than attempt a seL4-class refinement that cost **12 person-years for 8,700 lines of C** ([Klein, NICTA 4180](https://www.trustworthy.systems/publications/nicta_full_text/4180.pdf)). Where the notes disagreed, the reconciled positions are: canonical JSON stays the normative hash input and CBOR is at most a bijective transport mirror; C.6 counts and per-target joule vectors are two objects, both in D, never merged; and E is finite by VALUE_LIMIT but astronomically large, so finiteness buys decidability and nothing else.

## 1. Canonical forms: the minimal machine is a theorem for control and a fantasy for data

The hypothesis "every bounded Form is a finite Mealy machine; the minimal machine under name-free labeling is a canonical form of behavior" has a true first clause and a second clause that is true in theory and uncomputable at RCE's state sizes. For complete, deterministic machines over a finite alphabet the minimal machine is unique up to isomorphism, and a fixed first-visit traversal from the initial state with symbols in a fixed order turns "up to isomorphism" into a unique string: Reis, Moreira and Almeida prove a one-to-one mapping between canonical strings and non-isomorphic initially-connected DFAs, which FAdo uses to test isomorphism of minimal automata ([Reis–Moreira–Almeida, DCFS 2005](https://www.dcc.fc.up.pt/~nam/resources/publica/dcfsrma05.pdf)). RCE's T1 and T2 are precisely the two adjectives that make this work; canonicity comes from determinism plus completeness plus a fixed traversal order, not from minimality. The third adjective fails: a Form's alphabet includes i64 and text payloads bounded only by VALUE_LIMIT, so |E| is on the order of 256^(2^20) per event type, and explicit enumeration is out. The scale ceiling for symbolic enumeration is set by Sigref, which reduced a **4,224,876,912-state** LTS to a 68,217-state quotient in **53,725 s** on 2006 hardware ([Wimmer et al., ATVA 2006](https://ira.informatik.uni-freiburg.de/~wimmer/pubs/wimmer-et-al-atva-2006.pdf)); a single i64 field is that number squared.

The tractable fragment is exactly the one the symbolic-automata and register-automata literature delineates. Symbolic finite automata over an effective Boolean algebra of predicates have a minimal form unique "up to renaming states and up to equivalence of predicates", computable in O(n² log n · f(nℓ)) solver calls without minterm blow-up ([D'Antoni–Veanes, POPL 2014](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/sfaMinimizationPOPL14.pdf)); single-valued symbolic transducers over a decidable label theory have decidable equivalence in O(n²·f(m)), while symbolic transducers with registers are undecidable "already for the single-valued case, through direct encoding of 2-counter machines" ([Veanes et al., POPL 2012](https://www.doc.ic.ac.uk/~livshits/papers/pdf/popl12.pdf)). Register automata over equality or dense order admit a Myhill–Nerode theorem and a unique minimal well-typed DRA, but the integer signature with addition is called pathological ([Bojańczyk–Klin–Lasota, LICS 2011](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/bartek.klin/papers/lics11.pdf)), and even in the well-typed case a minimal DRA need not exist because states trade off against registers ([Li–Tang–Yen, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2605.03535)). The mapping onto RCE is clean: Σc (control regions, kind variants, bounded enums) plus the guard predicates of guarded updates is the SFA fragment with a unique canonical minimal machine; fields that are only compared and copied, never computed on, are the well-typed DRA fragment where that machine is exact behavior; the moment δ does i64 arithmetic into state, the only canonical object is the term itself. RCE's bounds move that last case from undecidable to decidable-in-time-exponential-in-VALUE_LIMIT, which is the same thing operationally.

Two cheaper decision procedures sit below the SFA quotient. GKAT's equational theory is "(almost) linear time" against KAT's PSPACE-completeness, and its automaton model is a deterministic coalgebra of size O(|e|) ([Smolka et al., POPL 2020](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1907.05920.pdf)); a Form's step body with guards as tests and guarded updates as uninterpreted actions is a GKAT expression, so its minimized automaton is a canonical form of the control skeleton for almost nothing. Above the quotient sits bounded translation validation: Alive2 checks refinement in Z3 at one minute and 1 GB per query, and raising the limit to five minutes bought under 5% more proofs, which says timeouts are structural ([Lopes et al., PLDI 2021](https://www-old.cs.utah.edu/~regehr/alive2-pldi21.pdf)). The e-graph is the partial canonicalizer in between: it decides exactly the EUF congruence of the admitted rules, so two bodies in one e-class are provably equal and two bodies in different classes are not known to differ. Extraction under DAG cost is NP-hard and inapproximable to any constant factor, while tree cost is polynomial and treewidth-bounded extraction runs in 2^O(w²)·poly ([Sun–Zhang–Ni, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.17042); hardness summarised in [Chugh, Jul 2026](https://mohakchugh.is-a.dev/blog/egraph-extraction-dag-cost-cycles-treewidth)). For a hash, the representative only needs to be deterministic, so tree cost with a canonical-JSON tie-break is the right extractor; the least-joule term is a different function over the same e-class.

Nothing in 2023–2026 gives a canonical form for statecharts or durable workflows; Temporal, Dapr and Lambda durable functions converge on deterministic replay as an operational constraint on user code and version by explicit markers. RCE is ahead on the theorem side and at parity on canonical forms, which means the research opening is precisely the computable fragment above.

## 2. Content-addressed IR: a Merkle DAG of α-erased normalised bodies, hashed as canonical JSON

Unison is the five-year experiment and its retrospective is unambiguous: the invariant that survived is "hash covers semantics, never names" (variables positional, dependencies by hash, names in a separate table), and the pain was all in re-hashing under edit, which needed a dependents index and a typed propagation algorithm before `update` was usable in 2023 ([Unison, The Big Idea](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/); [Unison, new update process, 2023-10-20](https://unison-lang.org/blog/new-update-process)). Dhall is the success story for content addressing normal forms: its semantic integrity hash is SHA-256 over CBOR of the fully resolved, αβ-normalised expression, with platform-fixed float encodings so "identical semantic hashes on different platforms" ([dhall-lang binary.md](https://github.com/dhall-lang/dhall-lang/blob/master/standard/binary.md)). Nix is the cautionary tale for content-addressing outputs: self-references made it "quite impossible" to hash before the build, non-determinism produced a diamond where two hashes of one derivation duplicate a library and "the C linker will fail", and the feature stayed experimental for fifteen years ([Tweag, 2020-09-10](https://www.tweag.io/blog/2020-09-10-nix-cas/); [Tweag, 2020-11-18](https://tweag.io/blog/2020-11-18-nix-cas-self-references)). Format 2's `defs` keyed by digest of the body sits squarely on the Dhall side, and its rule (a) forbids a body containing its own digest, so the Nix problem cannot arise; the only thing to add is that no node attribute or oracle key may ever feed back into the body digest.

Format 2 takes Dhall's lesson only half-way: keys are digests of the body as lowered, so two β-equivalent bricks that are not byte-equal get two entries. The fix is to hash after a fixed, confluent normaliser (ANF, sorted commutative arguments, α-erasure) and keep the e-graph representative as a second, semantic key. Full β-normalisation before hashing, which Dhall does, is wrong for RCE: Dhall can inline everything because it is total and non-recursive, and total inlining is exactly format 1's failure mode where "a recursive function has no finite term at all" and **77 of 188** stdlib refusals were for size or depth alone. Mutual recursion is the open seam: rule (a) makes the DAG acyclic by construction, Unison hashes a strongly connected component as one unit with positional member indices, and RCE's planned bounded `fix` with a decreasing measure is the Form-consistent alternative; whether a component-level key is compatible with the measure requirement is unproved.

On the encoding, every mature canonical format makes the same six decisions and the two ecosystems that skipped them tell users not to hash the bytes: Protobuf's own guidance is "we recommend against generic fingerprinting on messages" because unknown fields make canonicalisation impossible ([protobuf.dev](https://protobuf.dev/programming-guides/serialization-not-canonical/)). RFC 8949's deterministic CBOR sorts map keys by bytewise order of their encodings and uses the shortest float that preserves the value, while DAG-CBOR mandates 64-bit floats and length-first key order, so the two canonical CBORs disagree with each other on exactly the float rule RCE has already fixed ([RFC 8949 §4.2](https://www.rfc-editor.org/rfc/rfc8949#section-4.2); [IPLD DAG-CBOR](https://ipld.io/specs/codecs/dag-cbor/spec/)). RFC 8785 cannot represent int≠float, −0.0 or exact u64, which is why §6.1.1 diverges from it in six documented rows. RCE's canonical JSON already satisfies the structural checklist; what it pays is bytes (decimal floats and quoted keys are 2–5× CBOR on term trees), parse cost on every well-formedness check, and no native bytes type. None of those is a correctness argument for switching. The real integrity hazard is four hand-written runtimes (rt.rs 2084, rt.py 2107, rt.js 2000, rt.scala 1903 lines) each re-implementing the §6.1.1 serialiser and the totalOrder comparator; any drift there changes trace hashes silently across targets.

Losslessness has two readings and the paper uses both. Injective serialisation of the Form is a theorem; the lens round trip through a target language is a construction that can only hold on the image of the projection, because a well-behaved lens exists only when the source carries no information the view drops ([Foster et al., TOPLAS 2007](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1232420.1232424); [Hofmann–Pierce–Wagner, POPL 2011](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.1145/1926385.1926428)). The standard BX answer is a complement: an unhashed `src` side table keyed by node id holding spans, literal spellings and dropped constructs, so λ_L∘π_L = id on the shape hash by construction and a separate presentation hash covers the complement. The paper's own L0 data motivates this: **108 of 280** one-token misses were float-literal digit mutations, which is a leaf-level losslessness failure when `1.50` normalises to `1.5` ([RCE paper §6](https://research.openie.dev/papers/rce/)). WebAssembly's Canonical ABI is the closest published artefact to the emitter contract RCE needs: a single machine-readable table of type → core representation per target, with explicit NaN canonicalisation, narrow-int folding and surrogate-free string policy, and a deterministic profile that is the mode RCE already runs in ([CanonicalABI.md](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/WebAssembly/component-model/main/design/mvp/CanonicalABI.md)). Today the four emitters walk the surface AST, not the Form IR, through roughly 360 `Lang::` match arms, so the lens laws are asserted about an IR the emitters never see.

## 3. Partial-order semantics: ticks are Foata layers and the hash must stop depending on the linearization

A Mazurkiewicz trace is a word modulo commutation of independent events; linearizations of a configuration of the corresponding prime event structure all read off the same trace, and the configuration poset under inclusion is isomorphic to the trace structure under prefix order ([Thiagarajan, BRICS 1996](https://tidsskrift.dk/brics/article/download/20012/17645)). W-PAR-1's footprint disjointness is an independence relation, and the per-tick rule "rank order then name-free key" is a Foata-style normal form that picks one representative deterministically. So the question was never sequence versus DAG; it is whether the hash chain and the state hash are invariant across representatives of the same trace. Today they are not: `prev_hash` chains the chosen linearization. A tick is a layer of pairwise-independent events, which is a Foata layer, so the tick semantics is already a canonical representative, provided the ops in a tick commute; they do for channel ops with staged delivery and for `par`, and they do not for `select` on channels whose readiness depends on the staging order of earlier ticks.

CALM fixes which constructs survive a partial-order log: "a program has a consistent, coordination-free distributed implementation if and only if it is monotonic", and the cost of ignoring it is that contended key-value stores "can spend 90% of their time waiting for coordination" while Anna ran "over two orders of magnitude faster by eliminating that coordination" ([Hellerstein & Alvaro, CACM 2020](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1901.01930)). The extension RCE lacks is on reads: "CRDT guarantees extend only to data updates; observations of CRDT state are unconstrained and unsafe", and only monotone, threshold-shaped queries are safe without coordination ([Laddad et al., PVLDB 2022](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol16/p856-power.pdf)). Flo generalises monotonicity to eager execution, where any delta insertion point reaches the same stuck state, and makes it a per-operator obligation that composes; LVar threshold reads must be monotone or "there could be non-determinism due to when the threshold is evaluated", and fold is valid only over bounded inputs ([Laddad et al., POPL 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2411.08274)). Under that criterion `par`, `pscan`, workgroups, LVar `put`/`wait`, monotone rules and per-pair session FIFOs survive; `select`, `freeze`, `atomic`, `resolve`, supervisor restarts and deadlines do not and are the events that must be ordered, with deps on a cut. Concurrent Revisions supplies the missing theory for `atomic` and `with budget … else`: fork snapshots, join merges with per-location isolation types, every pair of states has a unique greatest common ancestor, and determinacy holds provided no revision is joined twice and merge functions "must not depend on nondeterministic details such as the particular identifiers appearing in values" ([Burckhardt et al., OOPSLA 2010](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/revisions-oopsla2010.pdf); mechanised in [Overbeek, 2019](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1912.09741)), which is RCE's name-free rule derived independently.

Every production replay system logs exactly the nondeterministic choices and re-derives the rest, which is E.2c law 1 in other words. rr places context switches by retired-conditional-branch count and records only syscall results, signals and RDTSC, at under 2× overhead ([O'Callahan et al., 2017](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1705.05937)); Temporal records `GetVersion` markers in history and a workflow "that has already passed this `GetVersion()` call before it was introduced returns `DefaultVersion`", with the pre-2025 versioning scheme slated for removal in March 2026 ([Temporal Go versioning](https://docs.temporal.io/develop/go/versioning)); TigerBeetle executes "all transfers one-by-one, on a single core" inside a simulator at 1000× speed on 1,024 cores ([TigerBeetle safety](https://docs.tigerbeetle.com/concepts/safety/)); Antithesis runs non-deterministic software inside a deterministic hypervisor descended from FoundationDB's simulation ([Antithesis DST](https://antithesis.com/docs/resources/deterministic_simulation_testing/)). Elle shows the partial order can be recovered from observations rather than trusted: with append-only objects every read reveals a version-order prefix, and anomalies are cycles found by Tarjan SCC in O(V+E) ([Kingsbury & Alvaro, PVLDB 2020](https://ar5iv.arxiv.org/html/2003.10554)). That is the cheap conformance check for a partial-order D. Pony is the warning on actors: causal delivery within a node gives data-race freedom but "message ordering stays non-deterministic where no causal relationship exists" ([Clebsch, 2017](https://www.ponylang.io/media/papers/codesigning.pdf)); RCE's actors are deterministic only because the global tick totally orders deliveries, so under a DAG log the mailbox must be defined as the HC-15 canonical merge of per-sender FIFOs at each cut.

## 4. Synthesis from intent: holes close by pruning before enumeration, and the gate must measure completeness

Everything that scales in the no-model regime scales by pruning before enumeration. SyGuS-Comp 2018 had CVC4 solving 448 of 598 general-track benchmarks and 724 of 750 PBE-BV, while no solver solved any instruction-selection benchmark and "multiple functions" stayed open ([SyGuS-Comp 2018](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/1904.07146)); that unsolved class maps exactly onto RCE's largest refusal class, calls inlined because the IR has no named sub-terms, so the `defs` table of §2 is a prerequisite for multi-function synthesis, not an optimisation. Hoogle+'s Petri-net encoding of a 291-component library broke on polymorphism and higher-order components; ECTA replaced it with tree automata carrying equality constraints between subterm paths and went from 64% to 88% on the combined suites with a tenth of the code, solving 13 of 19 higher-order queries where Hoogle+ solved none ([Koppel et al., ICFP 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2206.07828)). RCE's 457-brick catalog is the same order of magnitude and has generics, so it will hit the same wall; the type-directed stage should be ECTA-shaped. Burst's loop, where angelic assumptions become strengthened spec, is the structural twin of "repair is search with learned exclusions" and solves 94% of its suite ([Burst, POPL 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2107.06253)). G2 at handler-grammar size ≤2 is far below all of this.

The 2025–2026 numbers on verification-gated generation set the escalation policy. Agents complete over 80% of 849 real-system Verus proof tasks ([VeruSAGE, Dec 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.18436v1)) and over 90% of 150 AutoVerus tasks, half in under 30 s or three calls ([AutoVerus, 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2409.13082v1)), but synthesis of implementation plus proof from a formal spec runs **Dafny 67–82%, Verus 44%, Lean 27%** ([Vericoding, Sep 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2509.22908)). The decisive result is SpecRL's: the verifier checks only soundness, `ensures true` and `requires false` verify, and completeness has to be measured by spectests, mutated outputs the deterministic implementation can never produce; run as executable predicates they were ~8× faster than symbolic obligations, and removing the verification gate dropped verifiability to 1.56% ([SpecRL, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2604.05820)). Clover's three-way consistency of code, docstring and annotations accepted 87% of correct examples with zero false positives on adversarial ones ([Clover, 2023–24](https://arxiv.org/abs/2310.17807v4)). On ranking, entropy-delta from a small code LM raised top-1 correct plausible patches on Defects4J from 85 to 127 (+49%) and cut TBar's patches tested by a median of 5 per bug, though it lowered one component's ranking, so it is a ranker, never a gate ([Yang et al., CMU 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2404.15236)). Cost-of-pass, inference cost divided by pass rate, is the right accounting unit; majority voting and self-refinement "rarely justify their added cost" ([Erol et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.13359v1)). The benchmark warning is quantitative: top models sit at 80.9% on SWE-bench Verified and 46–57% on SWE-bench Pro, and an OpenAI audit found frontier models reproducing verbatim gold patches ([leaderboard compilation, Mar 2026](https://aicodereview.cc/blog/swe-bench-scores-leaderboard)). For provenance, cvc5 now emits fine-grained textual proof certificates at "a small runtime overhead" for skeptical checkers ([Tinelli, SAT 2024](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.SAT.2024.2)), which turns the gate's certificate from a verdict bit into a checkable object. AlphaEvolve's production wins came where the evaluator was exact or an external verifier existed ([DeepMind, May 2025](https://deepmind.google/discover/blog/alphaevolve-a-gemini-powered-coding-agent-for-designing-advanced-algorithms/)), and DGM's archive took two weeks and retained basic editing capability in only 51.3% of generated agents ([Zhang et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2505.22954v3)); both support scored ranking inside a gear and a proof gate at promotion.

## 5. Energy cost models: two regimes, one calibrated vector per target, and no counter is ever `measured_j`

The measurement literature says what every counter is worth. Intel RAPL on Haswell and later correlates with wall power at r = 0.99 and predicts plug power to **1.8% MAPE** once DRAM is included, but updates every ~1 ms non-atomically, overflows in 32 bits, and drifted 10–12% with package temperature on Haswell ([Khan et al., TOMPECS 2018](https://helda.helsinki.fi/server/api/core/bitstreams/bdc6c9a5-74d4-494b-ae83-860625a665ce/content)); polling it below 10 ms produces up to 50% stale reads and a 6.1% energy shift ([Dauner et al., HotCarbon 2026](https://hotcarbon.org/assets/2026/paper-46.pdf)); measurement itself raised idle power by up to 48.6 W on a Xeon E5-2680 v4 by killing deep C-states ([Raffin & Trystram, 2024](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2401.15985)). AMD RAPL is modeled, not measured, has no DRAM domain, and was blind to a 7.6% full-system power change from operand weights on 256-bit XOR ([Schöne et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2108.00808)). NVML on A100/H100 samples ~25% of runtime; naive single runs erred by up to ~70% against an external meter, and a phase-shifting protocol brings that to ~5% ([Yang et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/html/2312.02741v3)); RTX 4090 cumulative counters under-report by 75.6% at 10 ms ([HotCarbon 2026](https://hotcarbon.org/assets/2026/paper-46.pdf)). Jetson INA3221 under-reads by up to 50% with a near-constant per-device gap ([Shalavi et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2306.13107)); Apple powermetrics has no published validation. SPECpower runs 240 s per load level and MLPerf Power requires ≥60 s with SPEC-approved analyzers ([SPECpower overview](https://www.spec.org/power/docs/SPECpower_ssj2008-Design_Overview.pdf); [MLPerf Power](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2410.12032)). The receipts lattice `est < reported < measured` is correct in shape and too coarse in resolution: it cannot tell a 1.8%-MAPE counter from a 50%-under-reading one.

Per-op models work where the core is simple. A six-counter linear model on Cortex-M0 reaches R² ≈ 0.99 and 2.80–4.33% MAPE with coefficients like 0.964 nJ per instruction and 2.092 nJ per taken branch ([Lee et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2104.01055)); an ISA model lifted to LLVM IR gives 3.4% on XMOS but a direct IR model gives 9.9% mean and +28% worst on Cortex-M3 because IR-to-ISA expansion and predication break additivity ([Grech et al.](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1405.4565)). On a big core the model collapses to time: with one core fixed and turbo off, Xeon Gold 6430 power was **189.8 ± 0.5 W across all implementations and benchmarks**, DRAM was 2–8% of CPU energy, and the Pereira language ranking that put Python at 75.88× C disappeared once library choice, parallelism, warm-up and measurement bugs were controlled ([van Kempen et al., 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2410.05460v4)). What transfers across generations is ordering, not numbers: DRAM access stayed at 1300 pJ from 45 nm to 7 nm while FP32 fell 3.7 → 1.31 pJ and Int32 0.1 → 0.03 pJ ([Lee & Bruck, 2026, citing Horowitz and Jouppi](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2603.26053); [Horowitz, ISSCC 2014](https://pages.cs.wisc.edu/~markhill/restricted/isscc2014_horowitz_power_scaling.pdf)). Gas-style unit metering correlates with CPU time at only Pearson ≈ 0.51 on Ethereum, with DIV costing the same gas as MUL at ~5× the time ([Perez & Livshits](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1909.07220v2)); nothing in the literature correlates a C.6-style table with joules, so "energy scales with the operations performed" is an assumption the ladder's `reported_j` subsample has to test. WebAssembly interpreters on microcontrollers cost 11–52× native energy, so an interpreter fabric needs its own coefficient class ([Has et al., Nov 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2512.00035)). Landauer sits ~10⁹ below any CMOS op and the first reversible-adiabatic test chip cut energy by ~30% on one computation in August 2025 ([Science News](https://www.sciencenews.org/?p=3160033)); neither will ever move an extraction decision.

Extraction tooling constrains the objective's shape. egg's `CostFunction` computes a node's cost from its children and should be monotonic; TENSAT prices each operator by its measured runtime on the target and keeps the sum additive, and its ILP needed cycle pre-filtering to go from >3,600 s to 0.32 s ([Yang et al., TENSAT](https://www.arxiv.org/pdf/2101.01332)); SmoothE handles differentiable non-additive costs 8–37× faster than ILP and within 0–5% of a 10-hour CPLEX oracle on most sets, but 17% worst-case on tensat, while greedy was 12–53% off on average ([SmoothE, ASPLOS 2025](https://www.csl.cornell.edu/~zhiruz/pdfs/smoothe-asplos2025.pdf)); exact ASP extraction needs integer costs ([Yang & Sergey, 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2606.10644)). A per-target cost is therefore a coefficient vector over the op alphabet, priced in integer pJ, with target choice a swap of the vector, and Prop. 3's "least-joule term the extractor finds" is honest only once the extractor's optimality class is named.

## 6. The 2025–26 agent-language field: 42 entries, nine months old, and one defensible differentiator

agentlanguages.dev lists 42 languages in syntactic, verification and orchestration camps as of 19 October 2026: two from 2023, four from 2025, 36 from 2026, almost all single-author with under 100 stars; the industrially backed entries are MoonBit, Zero (Vercel Labs, 5.4k stars), Fabro, NanoLang, Vera and the dormant Marsha ([agentlanguages.dev](https://agentlanguages.dev)). The catalogue is curated by Vera's author, whose essay calls verification "the most crowded" camp and doubts the syntactic camp because failures are "errors of intent and coherence at scale, not tokenization errors" ([Three camps, May 2026](https://negroniventurestudios.com/2026/05/20/three-camps-alike-in-dignity/)). Every one of RCE's mechanisms has a peer: hash-chained evidence bundles with VM-enforced effects and claimed bit-identical replay (Boruna, 34 commits, unverified), mandatory contracts with an honest proven-versus-runtime tier split and De Bruijn slot references (Vera, with its own admission that "a naming bug class appeared anyway" in six subsystems), mandatory shadow tests and a Coq-proved core of 193 theorems with effects and codegen explicitly out of scope (NanoLang), AST-as-source-of-truth with BLAKE3-identified definitions and names in a sidecar (Tacit), and structured diagnostics with typed repair plans under the slogan "Humans read the message. Agents read the JSON." (Zero) ([Vera](https://agentlanguages.dev/languages/vera/); [Boruna](https://agentlanguages.dev/languages/boruna/); [NanoLang](https://agentlanguages.dev/languages/nanolang/); [Tacit](https://agentlanguages.dev/languages/tacit/); [Zero](https://agentlanguages.dev/languages/zero/)). No entry combines a canonical injective form, a hash-chained log with proved replay, examples as admission gate, mechanised metatheory and multi-target emission; the README claims all five, and the combination is the differentiator provided the Lean scope is stated as candidly as NanoLang states its NanoCore scope. The sharper claim is that RCE's examples are typed event traces over the same semantics replay uses, so the oracle the agent cannot bypass is the program itself; no peer does this.

The empirical results on syntax are consistent and mostly negative for novelty. Low-resource languages pay a large penalty (StarCoderBase pass@1 30.6 Python vs 6.9 OCaml) that test-validated synthetic translations recover (OCaml to 19.9) ([MultiPL-T, OOPSLA 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.09895)), and in-context learning always helps ([No Silver Bullet, ICPC 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.19085)); VeraBench reached 98.7% with zero training data from a primer, but single-run and saturated ([vera-bench](https://github.com/aallan/vera-bench)). Agents in unfamiliar languages "repeatedly produce non-compiling code", "invent test inputs instead of trusting provided tests" and "prototype in Python" ([Tokenmaxxing, 2026](https://www.khoury.northeastern.edu/~arjunguha/main/papers/wu-tokenmaxxing.html)), which is the direct argument for examples as the gate. Stripping formatting cuts input tokens ~24.5% with no significant accuracy loss, but only 6.5% for Python because its syntax is whitespace ([Hidden Cost of Readability, 2025](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2508.13666)), so RCE's indentation surface forfeits a free saving that brace languages can strip; the Form JSON already serves the compact role. Grammar-only constrained decoding is insufficient: adding semantic masking raised validity from 48.7 to 73.7 on Qwen3-4B ([Projectional Decoding, FSE 2026](https://www.alphaxiv.org/abs/2605.30054.md)), so the checker and LSP, not the grammar, are the sampler that matters. Format restrictions on reasoning output hurt ([Tam et al., 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2408.02442)). Vericoding's 82/44/27 split says auto-active Dafny-style contracts with decidable predicates are what models discharge and Lean proofs must stay on the spec side ([Vericoding](https://arxiv.org/abs/2509.22908v1)).

The mainstream signal validates the surface's structural choices and warns about its growth. Swift 6's strict concurrency was rolled back to "approachable concurrency" in 6.2 because it "moved too fast before the language, ecosystem, and documentation were ready" ([InfoQ, Aug 2025](https://www.infoq.com/news/2025/08/swift62-approachable-concurrency)); Carbon's 0.1 slipped to "end of 2026 at the soonest" ([Carbon roadmap](https://github.com/carbon-language/carbon-lang/blob/trunk/docs/project/roadmap.md)); manual pages went Pascal 167 → Java 891 as newer languages extend older ones ([Singer et al., 2014](https://www.dcs.gla.ac.uk/~jsinger/pdfs/ple14.pdf)); Go's process is "slanted towards declining" with a ten-to-one benefit rule ([Griesemer, 2018](https://go.googlesource.com/blog/+/master%5E/_content/go2-here-we-come.article)). ## 7. Proof engineering: derive the executor, enumerate the TCB, stop counting theorems

The projects that closed the spec-versus-model gap did it by making one artifact the authored source. SpecTec generates the typeset spec "with formal definitions and prose pseudocode", a meta interpreter that passes 100% of the applicable Wasm test suite, and Coq definitions from one DSL; it found ten errors in five proposals, the Wasm CG adopted it on 27 March 2025 for all future editions, and its residue is instructive: numeric primitives and the text format "are currently still written manually" and decoding/validation still use the OCaml interpreter ([PLDI 2024](https://pldi24.sigplan.org/details/pldi-2024-papers/64/Bringing-the-WebAssembly-Standard-up-to-Speed-with-SpecTec); [webassembly.org, 2025-03-27](https://webassembly.org/news/2025-03-27-spectec/)). CakeML's proof-producing translator from HOL functions to CakeML AST is "recommended for most new verified code" over post-hoc proof, and the compiler bootstraps inside the logic ([cakeml.org](https://cakeml.org/)). Rust's 2026 goal is a literate executable spec with authoritative and non-authoritative sections, one FTE requested and unfunded ([goals.rust-lang.org](https://goals.rust-lang.org/2026/executable-spec.html)). RCE has the single-source property for the grammar only; chapters 5–8's typing and evaluation rules are prose plus an independent Rust checker plus an independent Lean core, which is the pre-SpecTec Wasm situation, and `core_rules.rs → Rules.lean` checks rule names, not content.

The cost record forecloses the refinement route. seL4's kernel proof was about 12 person-years of the 25, 200,000 lines of Isabelle for 8,700 lines of C, under $350 per line, with a redo estimated at 8–10 person-years by a team that had already built the frameworks ([Klein, NICTA 4180](https://www.trustworthy.systems/publications/nicta_full_text/4180.pdf); [seL4 numbers](https://trustworthy.systems/projects/OLD/l4.verified/numbers)). Verify-Rust-Std, a multi-tool community effort, reached contracts on **3.98%** of `core`'s unsafe functions after about sixteen months, found two specification bugs and no safety bugs, and reports that Kani results "hold only within finite scope" ([Le Blanc & Lam, Oct 2025](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.01072v2)); that is the realistic yield of RCE's 10 Kani harnesses, which should be reported as bounded with their unwind counts, not as verified. CompCert's maintainers enumerate 15 trusted items with a reason for each, and name unverified assembly printing and expansion as "the main source of bugs" ([Monniaux & Boulmé, ESOP 2022](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2201.10280)); Csmith's six CPU-years against CompCert found wrong-code bugs only in the unverified front end and the trusted assembler interface, never in the verified middle ([Yang et al., PLDI 2011](https://www-old.cs.utah.edu/~regehr/papers/pldi11-preprint.pdf)), which is to say differential fuzzing locates the proof boundary. Ferrocene qualifies rustc to ASIL D with a hazard → potential error → test traceability matrix ([Ferrocene evaluation report](https://public-docs.ferrocene.dev/main/qualification/evaluation-report/index.html)). On the Lean side, the FRO's September 2026 roadmap ships `lake check` (export and recheck with the official kernel), a comparator in the distribution, `grind` with `bv_decide`, and a new code generator implemented from early 2027 ([Lean FRO Y4-1](https://lean-lang.org/fro/roadmap/y4-1)); RCE is pinned to v4.12.0 and runs the S6 oracle through `lake env lean --run` because compiled 4.12 binaries do not load on current macOS. ML proof repair is a 127-problem workshop benchmark, not tooling ([ICLR 2026](https://www.iclr.cc/virtual/2026/10012643)).

## Reconciled positions where the notes disagree with each other or with the paper

**Canonical JSON versus CBOR as the hash input.** The IR note considered deterministic CBOR and refused it; the canonical-forms note assumed canonical JSON throughout. The reconciled position is that §6.1.1 canonical JSON remains the single normative hash input and the only thing a digest is ever computed over. RFC 8785 cannot carry int≠float, −0.0 or u64; RFC 8949 and DAG-CBOR disagree on float width and key order, so adopting either would reopen the float rule that §6.1.1 already closed and that N3 (no NaN) depends on. JSON's costs are bytes, parse time and the absence of a bytes type, none of which touches correctness. A CBOR mirror is permitted only as a bijection to the §6.1.1 bytes, tested on the same 77 vectors, used for transport and never hashed. The actual risk is not the encoding but four independent serialisers in four runtimes; the fix is one shared serialiser (Rust via WASM for JS and Python, a generated JVM port) or a conformance gate per target in CI.

**Target-blind C.6 counts versus per-target cost vectors.** The energy note insists C.6 stays target-blind; the canonical-forms note treats `cost = est_j` as the extraction objective and separately wants a reproducible hash representative. These are three distinct functions over one e-class and the design must say so. C.6 budget units are deterministic, target-blind, in D, and exist to place the budget trap on the same operation in every runtime; they are never joules. `est_j` is `⟨count(term), E[target]⟩` plus the regime-B time term, with `E[target]` a `host.Calibrated` entry in D, so extraction under `est_j` is a function of D and replays (T37) even though it differs per target. The shape-hash representative (`shape_eq`) is a third function: tree cost with canonical-JSON tie-break, chosen for reproducibility, not for joules. The emitted term and the hashed term of one e-class therefore legitimately differ; what must not differ is either one across engines given the same D.

**Finite versus astronomically large E.** The paper's "bounded state, events" reads as finite; the canonical-forms note first says E is "not finite as specified", then corrects itself. The reconciled statement: S and E are finite by VALUE_LIMIT (N9) and SIZE_LIMIT, with |S| ≤ 256^VALUE_LIMIT and |E| ≤ (event types) · 256^VALUE_LIMIT. Finiteness makes every behavioral question decidable and makes none of them tractable; the practical content of "every Form is a finite Mealy machine" is the Σc-plus-predicates projection, and the paper should say "finite, astronomically large, canonical only on the finite-control fragment" rather than "finite".

**Total-order hash chain versus partial-order log.** The agent-language note names the hash chain plus the proved fold law as RCE's differentiator; the partial-order note wants a DAG. Both hold: admission keeps the admitter's canonical total order and HC-15 key, so T3 and Prop. 4 survive unchanged along that linearization, while entry hashes become linearization-independent by hashing deps instead of `prev_hash`, and state hashes move to cuts. The differentiator becomes stronger, not weaker: the theorem to add is that every linearization consistent with deps folds to the same cut state.

**De Bruijn in the hash versus De Bruijn in the surface.** The IR note adopts α-erasure before hashing; the field note refuses De Bruijn-only surfaces and cites Vera's naming bugs returning in tooling. No conflict: positions inside the IR and the hash (T7 already), names in the surface and in an unhashed table, never slots in source.

**Hashing the normalised body versus the as-lowered body.** Format 2 as proposed keys bodies as lowered; the IR note wants the normalised body; the canonical-forms note wants an e-class representative. Resolved by the three tiers: the store key is the digest of the α-erased, ANF-normalised body (`shape_syn` at the `defs` granularity), and `shape_ctl`/`shape_eq` are coarser semantic keys computed from it. Adding catalog laws changes `shape_eq` without invalidating stored keys.

**Always-on contracts versus static discharge.** The synthesis note keeps F.6.2's runtime enforcement; the field note observes that models discharge Dafny-style auto-active contracts at 82% and Lean at 27%. Compatible: the surface stays Dafny-shaped with decidable refinements, the checker discharges statically what the decidable fragment decides, everything else is enforced at runtime, and `est_j` charges the real predicate cost rather than one operator per clause; the only change is that "one comparison per clause" is dropped as a cost claim.

## The unified design

**The Form.** P = (S, E, δ, D, λ) stays, with three additions. Σ is split by declaration into a control component Σc (regions, kinds, bounded enums, booleans) and a data component Σd (i64/u64/f64, text, lists, maps), and each data field carries a derived usage class, `compared` (only `==`/`<` and copies) or `computed`; the class is derived, sound, and refused if a stored class disagrees. Behavioral equivalence is defined once as bisimulation on the product Form over (S, O*) with `trap` and `Refused` in the output alphabet. The monotone tag applies to reads as well as rules, using Flo's eager-execution criterion as its definition. S and E are stated as finite and astronomically large; the Mealy-machine claim is stated for the Σc-plus-guard-predicates projection and proved there.

**The log.** An entry is `{chain, seq, event, deps, hash}` with the hash over canonical JSON of those four fields, one chain per source, actor or task, deps the hashes the event causally observed. `state_hash` appears only at cuts. The admitter's HC-15 key remains the normative linearization for admission; RESOLVE and handler install/retire are cut entries; multi-chain `select` arms and every other non-monotone choice are logged, derivable entries checked on replay as HC-6 checks `host.Step`. Replay refuses an entry whose deps are not all earlier and refuses a D whose recovered dependency graph has a cycle. Compaction produces a snapshot entry whose hash covers the folded state and the frontier it replaces, before RCEdb is promoted. `host.Calibrated` entries carry per-target coefficient vectors with provenance, so pricing is replayable.

**The IR.** Format 2's `defs` as a Merkle DAG; keys are SHA-256 of canonical JSON of the α-erased, ANF-normalised body; names, spans, literal spellings and dropped source constructs live in unhashed side tables (`names`, `src`) covered by a separate presentation hash. Three shape-hash tiers, each naming its tier and, for `shape_eq`, its saturation budget, in the hashed input. The e-graph treats `ref` as an opaque leaf carrying its definition's laws and contracts as e-class analyses, inlines only under INLINE_MAX, is bounded in nodes and iterations like LOOP_LIMIT, and runs a deterministic scheduler. Mutual recursion waits on either component-level keys or single-body `fix`; nothing else is admitted into the DAG. One emitter walks the Form IR with a per-target lowering table in the Canonical ABI's shape; every target emits function names from the key; every runtime that serialises passes the 77 canonical-JSON vectors in CI, ideally by sharing one serialiser.

**The surface.** The five-source surface stays but gets a Go-style decline-biased proposal rule, a stated budget on keywords and W-rules, a per-version removed-features line and `rce migrate`. Contracts stay Dafny-shaped with decidable refinements and are always enforced where not statically discharged. Names stay in the surface; positions stay in the hash. The invariant that tests every proposal is stated once: an error is an output in λ and an event in D, never an unlogged throw; nothing is hidden control flow, nothing is a hidden allocation. The agent interface is table stakes: a versioned rule-code registry, `--json` diagnostics, `rce fix --plan`, `rce explain`, a byte-pinned `rce primer --section`. The indentation trade is stated and the Form JSON is the compact serialisation for agent contexts. `rce emit` and `rce test` manufacture test-validated translation pairs as the training and few-shot corpus.

**The gate.** Admission stays replay plus shadow run plus schema check plus certificate, and adds three things: a stored proof certificate object (cvc5/Z3 textual) rather than a verdict; a measured intent completeness (spectest rejection fraction under a seeded mutation of recorded outputs) with W-INT-1 refusing vacuous intents; and Clover-style consistency between intent prose, examples/ensures and body whenever a model gear proposes. Every rewrite enters the e-graph only with a proof that includes the overflow trap in the output alphabet; floats remain syntactic. Ranking inside a gear is free to use scores (entropy-delta, fitness); promotion never is. The derivation log records, per hole, every eliminating check with candidates-before and candidates-after, so search bits are attributed by stage and the gate's contribution is a number. Escalation across gears minimises expected cost-of-pass; self-refinement inside a gear is not used. L5–L7 report plausible and correct separately on held-out, multi-language tasks with a contamination falsifier.

**The cost model.** Three objects, never merged: C.6 budget units (deterministic, target-blind, in D, trap-placing); `est_j` under regime A (additive per-op vector) or regime B (time model with memory and idle terms), with the coefficient vector a `host.Calibrated` entry carrying SE and provenance; and the label lattice `unpriced < est < reported < measured` with `reported_j` sub-classed by validation and never summed across labels. The extraction objective is linear and additive per target, priced in integer pJ, run by a DAG-aware extractor; non-additive effects live in calibration. The calibration protocol is normative (interleaved randomized pairs, ≥60 s or ≥10⁹ evaluations per block, warm-up, temperature, idle baseline, gross and net, estimate beside reading). The falsifier is the regression of `reported_j` on op counts per target with published R². Thermodynamic bounds appear on receipts only as labeled bounds. The energy claim for coordination-freeness is structural until `est_j` receipts compare monotone and non-monotone workloads on one fabric.

**The proof discipline.** One authored rule source generates prose, checker tables and Lean definitions, with a three-way drift gate. The Lean core is the executor of record for its subset and ships as a compiled, hash-logged binary; `unsupported` counts per construct are published and cap theorem status labels; a grammar-driven semantic fuzzer runs reference, Lean and emitted targets differentially, because that is where CompCert's bugs were. Chapter 7's primitive tables are vector-governed from table rows to `by decide` Lean and Rust test rows, pinned by chapter hash. A TCB chapter lists every trusted item with its backing test. `lake check` pins "no sorry, three axioms" in CI and audit numbers are generated. Models stay in a separate table labelled design models. Kani harnesses are reported with unwind bounds. Nothing is refined from `RCE.Core` to `interp.rs` by hand; the executable is derived from the proved definition or the definition is generated into the executable.

## Conclusion

The pattern across all seven areas is that RCE's theorems are the right theorems and its claims outrun its computation in exactly three places: the canonical form (true for finite control, not for arithmetic state), the lens (a construction on the image, not a theorem on the source), and the least-joule extraction (true for the extractor's optimality class under a calibrated vector that does not yet exist). Each of those is fixed by naming the fragment, the complement, or the extractor rather than by retreating from the claim, and in each case the fix produces a new, sharper theorem to prove: the converse of T50 on the GKAT-plus-DRA fragment, linearization-invariance of the DAG fold at cuts, and an R² falsifier for every priced target.

The second-order implication is that the project's scarcest resource is not proof effort but authored sources. Every sync failure in the record, from pre-SpecTec Wasm to the paper's own three disagreeing theorem counts, came from two hand-written artifacts that were supposed to mean the same thing. A solo builder cannot afford one refinement proof, but can afford one rule table, one serialiser, one lowering table and one executor, each generating everything downstream; that is the shape of a language whose correctness is proven rather than reviewed, and it is the shape nothing in the 2026 cohort has yet built.
