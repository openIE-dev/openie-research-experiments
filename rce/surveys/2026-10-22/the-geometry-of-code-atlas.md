# The geometry of code atlas

The string-theory frame survives as one theorem, four precise analogies and three false pictures, and the theorem is not the one the author started from: behaviour space is not a manifold but the **final coalgebra of the Mealy functor (O × X)^E, a complete ultrametric space of causal stream functions E^ω → O^ω** that is totally disconnected and of topological dimension zero ([Rutten, TCS 2000](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00056-6); [de Bakker & Zucker 1982](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0019-9958(82)91013-8)). The Form P = (S, E, δ, D, λ) is then not a global chart on that space but a coalgebra whose unique behaviour map lands on the sub-space of total, bounded-exploration, sequential behaviours — exactly where Gurevich's sequential-ASM theorem applies ([Gurevich, TOCL 2000](https://doi.org/10.1145/343369.343384)) — and the only theorem-grade "dimension" of any program is the Nerode index of its minimal machine, which exists for finite control and nowhere else ([Nerode 1958](https://doi.org/10.2307/2033204)). Across the atlas of roughly a hundred languages in five lineages, every family fixes state and transition, frees order by default, and compactifies the log, intent and energy coordinates to a point; the five charts nearest the Form are Dedalus (relational S, time-indexed E, `@next` as δ, the relations themselves as D), Bluespec (guarded atomic rules with a proved one-rule-at-a-time schedule), the Elm architecture (Model/Msg/update is literally (S, E, δ, λ)), Lustre/SCADE (bounded memory, static schedule, about a hundred DO-178B/C level-A systems) and Unison (SHA3-512 of the name-free syntax tree as identity). The atlas imposes five hard constraints the earlier design document did not state: `spawn` must lower stackless (Rust RFC 2394's enum-per-yield-point) because stackful coroutines cannot be logged step-wise; `par` must be emitted as a structured step, never an explicit product automaton, because Esterel's automaton compilers died at about 1000 lines of concurrent source ([Benveniste et al. 2003](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~sedwards/papers/benveniste2003synchronous.pdf)); effects must be one-shot, as OCaml 5 and WasmFX chose, with the honest reason being ownership and the T2 bound rather than loggability ([Sivaramakrishnan et al. 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00250)); the Nerode index is the only dimension that is a theorem; and the lift from any Turing-complete source is a partial map whose domain membership is undecidable (Rice 1953, applied to a total target). The measurements that would turn the remaining analogies into numbers — the Nerode index of real corpora and the quantitative holonomy of any translation pair — have never been done, and the one measured holonomy in the literature is the 88–104× slowdown at typed/untyped boundaries in Typed Racket ([Takikawa et al., POPL 2016](https://doi.org/10.1145/2837614.2837630)).

## Foundations: one theorem, four analogies with precise replacements, three false pictures

The reconciliation starts by replacing the manifold with the object the literature already proved. A Mealy machine is a coalgebra for F(X) = (O × X)^E; the final coalgebra of F is the set of causal stream functions, every machine has a unique behaviour map into it, and two states are behaviourally equivalent iff they map to the same point ([Rutten, TCS 2000](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00056-6); Mealy-specific synthesis in [Bonsangue, Rutten & Silva, FoSSaCS 2008](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-540-78499-9_17)). That space carries the prefix ultrametric d(f, g) = 2^−n for the longest agreeing prefix, is complete, and admits semantics as unique Banach fixed points ([America & Rutten, JCSS 1989](https://doi.org/10.1016/0022-0000(89)90027-5)). It has no tangent spaces, no connection and no geodesics; it has a universal property instead, which is strictly more useful to a language whose Definition 3 ("same output sequence for every event sequence") is precisely equality of behaviour maps. The Reality Code Engine paper does not use the words manifold, chart, gauge, holonomy or geodesic ([RCE paper §2.1, §3, §3.6](https://research.openie.dev/papers/rce/)), so nothing in the normative text has to change; the working geometry does.

Three of the remaining pictures are theorems in bounded cases. "Translations are transition maps" is exact and invertible for the cycle SSA ↔ CPS ↔ ANF ↔ functional programs ([Kelsey, IR'95](https://doi.org/10.1145/202529.202532); [Appel 1998](https://doi.org/10.1145/278283.278285); [Flanagan et al., PLDI 1993](https://doi.org/10.1145/155090.155113)), for evaluator ↔ abstract machine via closure conversion, CPS and defunctionalization ([Ager, Biernacki, Danvy & Midtgaard, PPDP 2003](https://doi.org/10.1145/888251.888254)), for data ↔ codata ([Hagino 1987](https://www.lfcs.inf.ed.ac.uk/reports/87/ECS-LFCS-87-38/); [Downen et al., ESOP 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-030-17184-1_5)), for call-by-value ↔ call-by-name as a literal involution on sequent-calculus syntax ([Curien & Herbelin, ICFP 2000](https://doi.org/10.1145/351240.351262); [Wadler, ICFP 2003](https://doi.org/10.1145/944705.944723)), for sessions ↔ linear proofs ([Wadler, ICFP 2012](https://doi.org/10.1145/2364527.2364568)) and for game strategies ↔ abstract machines ([Danos, Herbelin & Regnier, LICS 1996](https://lics.siglog.org/1996/DanosHerbelinRegnie-Gamesemanticsandabs.html)). The inverse direction is conditional: refunctionalization exists exactly when the first-order program is "in defunctionalized form", one dispatch function per sum type ([Danvy & Millikin, BRICS RS-08-4, 2008](https://www.brics.dk/RS/08/4/)), and the CPS image is lossless on equational theories only after Sabry and Felleisen's axioms are added to the source ([Sabry & Felleisen 1993](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01019462)). "Duality" needs no qualification; the involutions above are all proved. And "the Form is a global chart" is a theorem on a sub-space: Gurevich's three postulates (sequential time, abstract state, bounded exploration) give step-for-step simulation by a sequential ASM ([TOCL 2000](https://doi.org/10.1145/343369.343384)), the Form adds totality and a fixed vocabulary, and the parallel extension rests on the Blass–Gurevich postulates that "have not fully convinced the ASM community" ([Blass & Gurevich, TOCL 2003](https://doi.org/10.1145/937555.937561); [Ferrarotti et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1504.06203)).

Four pictures are analogies with a precise replacement each. "Each language is a coordinate chart" has a theorem core — a compositional semantics is a functor from an initial algebra to behaviour ([Lawvere, PNAS 1963](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.50.5.869)) — but the map is many-to-one, so a language is a *cover* of its expressible sub-space and becomes an injective chart only after quotienting by program equivalence, which is a chart in the strict sense only when the model is fully abstract ([Plotkin, TCS 1977](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(77)90044-5); [Hyland & Ong 2000](https://doi.org/10.1006/inco.2000.2917)). Languages have different domains because features like first-class continuations and assignment are not macro-expressible ([Felleisen, SCP 1991](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-6423(91)90036-W)). "Rewrites form a gauge group with e-classes as orbits" becomes: an e-class is a connected component of the groupoid whose arrows are rewrite proofs, built by congruence closure ([Tate et al., POPL 2009](https://doi.org/10.1145/1480881.1480915); [Willsey et al., POPL 2021](https://doi.org/10.1145/3434304)); it is not a group action because rewrites are directed and equalities have many proofs, and in HoTT terms the e-class is a path-connected component whose non-trivial π₁ egg discards ([HoTT Book 2013](https://homotopytypetheory.org/book/)). The orbit is decidable exactly when the equivalence is: finite-state Mealy in n log n ([Hopcroft 1971](https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-12-417750-5.50022-1)), KAT in PSPACE ([Kozen, TOPLAS 1997](https://doi.org/10.1145/256167.256195); [Cohen, Kozen & Smith 1996](https://ecommons.cornell.edu/handle/1813/7253)), DPDA by Sénizergues ([TCS 2001](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00285-1)), and undecidable for unbounded data even in a total language, since Rice's theorem applies to Π₁ properties of total functions on unbounded domains ([Rice 1953](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0002-9947-1953-0053041-6)). Rovelli's reading that gauge variables are relational redundancies rather than nothing is the one conceptually apt use of the word ([Rovelli 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1308.5599)). "Round-trip loss is holonomy" becomes: in the groupoid whose objects are representations and arrows are translations, the holonomy at a Form P is the set of automorphisms of P's e-class induced by cycles L₁ → L₂ → … → L₁; flatness is impossible whenever any language in the cycle is non-injective on behaviour or strictly less expressive, so the lift/project round trip is lossless on the e-class and never on the representation. Lens laws GetPut/PutGet are retraction conditions, not transport ([Foster et al., TOPLAS 2007](https://doi.org/10.1145/1232420.1232424); [Hofmann, Pierce & Wagner, POPL 2011](https://doi.org/10.1145/1926385.1926428)); the paper's "canonical serialization is injective" gives PutGet for the Form's own serialisation and nothing for arbitrary sources, which is where the earlier design document's symmetric-lens complement already landed. "Languages compactify coordinates" is the weakest analogy: the information is discarded, not made periodic, so "integrate out" is the right physics word; the precise content is Felleisen's macro-expressibility plus the continuation coordinate, explicit in CPS and the Form and implicit in direct style ([Danvy, SCP 1994](https://doi.org/10.1016/0167-6423(94)90003-5)), with Filinski's result that continuations plus one mutable cell generate every monadic effect making continuations-and-state a universal coordinate system for effects ([Filinski, POPL 1994](https://doi.org/10.1145/174675.178047)).

Two pictures are false. "Cost is a metric and extraction is a geodesic" fails because a joule cost is a potential on representations and Proposition 3's extraction is argmin over a finite e-class with no path structure; the only true metrics in sight are information distance E(x,y) = max{K(x|y), K(y|x)}, universal and uncomputable ([Bennett et al., IEEE IT 1998](https://doi.org/10.1109/18.681318)), and the behavioural (ultra)metrics of quantitative equational logic ([Mardare, Panangaden & Plotkin, LICS 2016](https://doi.org/10.1145/2933575.2934518); [Dahlqvist & Neves, CSL 2022](https://doi.org/10.4230/LIPIcs.CSL.2022.16)). The defensible restatement is that least-joule extraction is MDL with joules in place of bits — shortest description under a model class ([Rissanen, Automatica 1978](https://doi.org/10.1016/0005-1098(78)90005-5)) — which is a theorem-grade correspondence that needs no geometry. "Moduli space" and "landscape" have no technical PL usage; the e-graph over a fixed behaviour is the nearest object and the Nerode minimal machine is its canonical point when one exists. No paper was found that applies holonomy, connections or compactification to program translation; the groupoid formalisation above is the author's construction and should be stated as such.

Two further results anchor the frame from the binding-time and information sides. The Futamura projections are theorems given a self-applicable specialiser, and binding-time analysis assigns every sub-expression a static/dynamic value ([Futamura 1971, HOSC 1999](https://doi.org/10.1023/A:1010095604496); [Jones, Gomard & Sestoft 1993](https://www.itu.dk/people/sestoft/pebook/)); MetaML types it ([Taha & Sheard, TCS 2000](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0304-3975(00)00053-0)); Truffle runs the first projection in production ([Würthinger et al., Onward! 2013](https://doi.org/10.1145/2509578.2509581)). Binding time is therefore a decidable gauge direction on representations, not on behaviour, and the Reality Code Engine's `comptime` plus `rce emit` is a hand-written first projection. On the information side, code is measurably lower-entropy than English ([Hindle et al., ICSE 2012](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSE.2012.6227135)), buggy lines are higher-entropy ([Ray et al., ICSE 2016](https://doi.org/10.1145/2884781.2884848)) and the signal survives into LLMs with a 50% Top-5 fault-localisation gain ([Yang et al. 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2404.15236)), but the raw gap shrinks substantially once syntax tokens are removed ([Rahman et al., ICSE 2019](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSE.2019.00022)) and part of the rest is vocabulary ([Casalnuovo et al., EMSE 2019](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10664-018-9669-7)). The paper's §2.1 "programming is coding theory" should cite both corrections. No published study measures an intrinsic dimension of code embeddings or a Nerode-style state count across corpora; the embedding-dimension work that exists is on NL token embeddings ([Lee et al. 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2503.21073)) or on code2vec feature importance without an ID estimate ([Rabin et al. 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/2008.13064)).

The universal-form precedents close the foundations. UNCOL promised n front-ends × m back-ends through one intermediate in 1958 and never produced one ([Strong et al., CACM 1958](https://doi.org/10.1145/368892.368915); [Steel 1961](https://doi.org/10.1145/1460690.1460733)); LLVM, WebAssembly and sea-of-nodes succeed by claiming only the lowering direction and discarding source structure ([Lattner & Adve, CGO 2004](https://doi.org/10.1109/CGO.2004.1281665); [Haas et al., PLDI 2017](https://doi.org/10.1145/3062341.3062363)); MLIR succeeds by abandoning the single-IR premise for dialects with explicit lowerings between them ([Lattner et al., CGO 2021](https://doi.org/10.1109/CGO51591.2021.9370308)), which is the industrial atlas with transition maps and explicitly not one global chart. Read through Felleisen, UNCOL's lesson is that a universal intermediate must sit at the least expressive point every source is macro-expressible into; the Form's propositions U2 (projection) are in the easy direction consistent with every precedent, and U3 (lift by construction) is the UNCOL direction and can only be partial.

## The atlas: twelve families, eight coordinates, one state-machine duality each

The notation across the five lineage notes is uniform: **fix** means the author must write the coordinate, **free** means compiler, runtime or machine chooses it, **pt** means the chart has no word for it (compactified). The coordinates are S (state layout), E (event alphabet), δ (transition), O (order/concurrency), R (resource/energy), T (trust/authority), B (binding time), I (intent/spec). Rows aggregate a family; the lineage notes carry the per-language rows (around a hundred languages) and the fetched/[bg] status of each. The last column is the duality that connects the family to a first-order state machine, which is the "FSM-in" transition map of the lineage notes.

Three patterns fall out of the table that no single lineage shows. First, only three families produce global determinism for free, and they do it by three different theorems: synchrony with a causality check, Kahn/CALM monotonicity, and one-rule-at-a-time with static conflict analysis. Actor systems give isolation, not determinism, and need a logged merge order to become Forms — which is what the content-hash `select` order in SURFACE.md supplies, at the cost that the hash order is a canonical but arbitrary serialisation rather than a causal one. Second, every family that achieved certification (SCADE, Ravenscar Ada, occam, synthesizable Verilog) did so by removing the same three coordinates the Form removes: dynamic allocation, unbounded recursion, nondeterministic choice. Third, the coordinates no lineage fixes well are T and I. Only Erlang supervisors, Pony capabilities, Austral linearity, Rust `unsafe` and Elm 0.19's refusal of kernel code touch T; only TLA+/Event-B/Alloy, SCADE observers, Ada/SPARK contracts, D and Prolog's declarative reading touch I. The Form's `intent`/`example` blocks and supervisor trees occupy both at once, which the earlier design document counted as the differentiator among 2026 agent languages and which the atlas now shows is also the differentiator against the sixty-year record.

The binding-time coordinate deserves its own reading because every "duality" in the ALGOL family is a binding-time phenomenon: async ≅ defunctionalized continuation, `comptime` ≅ offline partial evaluation ([Zig overview](https://ziglang.org/learn/overview/), fetched 2026-10-22), templates ≅ Turing-complete compile-time computation, Rust editions ≅ "skin deep" surface variants over one internal representation ([Rust Edition Guide](https://doc.rust-lang.org/edition-guide/editions/index.html)). In the ML family it is the coordinate with the widest variance and the least metatheory: no row has a proved-sound macro system beyond Lean's hygiene argument and MetaOCaml's typed staging. In the dynamic family it is freed all the way to run time, and the Lisps' failure to produce canonical *behaviour* from canonical *syntax* is exactly that: homoiconicity fixes text → tree to the identity and leaves tree → behaviour parameterised by expansion environment, phase and reader table, so Racket's stateless servlets need a six-step post-expansion rewrite and still invalidate every saved continuation when a constant changes ([Racket web-server](https://docs.racket-lang.org/web-server/stateless.html)). The Form canonicalises δ rather than the input of δ, which is the move the Lisps never made.

## Blind spots: each family's integrated-out coordinate, and the five nearest charts

Each family's blind spot is the coordinate it integrates out, and in every case the family's own rationale documents the cost. The systems family's blind spot is partial δ and free O: undefined behaviour in C, panics in Rust and Go, Illegal Behavior in Zig framed as "a razor sharp tool" ([Zig overview](https://ziglang.org/learn/overview/)); nondeterministic threads as the baseline everywhere except Ravenscar, and Swift 6.2 rolling strict concurrency back to "single-threaded by default" under user pressure ([Swift 6.2, 2025-09-15](https://www.swift.org/blog/swift-6.2-released/)). Its accidental dimensions are holonomy of the lowering: Rust's `Pin` exists because the compiler's own lowering creates self-referential frames and RFC 2394 lists making futures `Unpin` as unresolved; Swift 6.0's isolation annotations turned out to be accidental for most programs; Zig's allocator parameter makes R fixed even where behaviour is allocation-indifferent, and Odin makes the opposite choice with an implicit `context` ([Odin FAQ](https://odin-lang.org/docs/faq/), fetched 2026-10-22). The dynamic family's blind spot is the implicit, uniform error branch: δ is total only because there is always a `TypeError`, so refusal is unnamed; the measured cost of re-expanding the type coordinate soundly after the fact is 88–104× at typed/untyped boundaries, recovered to >90% by a tracing JIT, and resolved in production by making the coordinate a three-position dial (deep, shallow, optional) ([Takikawa et al. 2016, via Morning Paper](https://blog.acolyer.org/2016/02/05/is-sound-gradual-typing-dead/); [Bauman et al., OOPSLA 2017](https://scholarworks.iu.edu/dspace/items/131d8f23-1943-45ae-86c3-28a6a526f0e0); [Racket 8.7, 2022-11-20](https://blog.racket-lang.org/2022/11/shallow-typed-racket.html)). That overhead is proportional to the number of chart crossings, not program size, which makes it the only quantitatively measured holonomy in the atlas. The ML family's blind spot is R and I: laziness turns space into a non-coordinate (Fran's space leaks), totality survived only in config languages and provers, and the one fully total general-purpose design ([Turner, JUCS 2004](https://doi.org/10.3217/jucs-010-07-0751)) was never adopted; the cost is checker friction, not expressiveness, since partiality as a monadic effect loses nothing ([McBride, MPC 2015](https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-319-19797-5_13)). The synchronous family's blind spot is state explosion and the absence of data: Esterel's automaton compilers cannot handle concurrent programs beyond about a thousand lines, circuit translation keeps size linear but loses the explicit state graph and can run 100× slower ([Benveniste et al. 2003](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~sedwards/papers/benveniste2003synchronous.pdf)). The actor family's blind spot is the unlogged merge. The logic family's blind spot is the unobservable step count: Datalog has no notion of event order until Dedalus adds it, and CALM is "not constructive" ([Keeping CALM](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1901.01930)). The array family's blind spot is everything except S: nothing to say about events, order, trust or intent, which is why the one array language that won a market (q) did so by bolting on relations. The HDL family's blind spot is that cost is reported after a deterministic lowering and nowhere else; the fetched Bluespec sources report area and time but never energy.

Five existing charts are closest to the Form, and each is close on a different coordinate subset. **Dedalus** is closest on the (S, E, D) triple: state is relational, events are facts stamped with a time attribute, `@next` rules are δ, `@async` rules are externally arriving events chosen by `choice`, and the time-indexed relations are themselves the log; it "reduces to a subset of Datalog with negation, aggregate functions, successor and choice" ([Alvaro et al. 2009](https://www2.eecs.berkeley.edu/Pubs/TechRpts/2009/EECS-2009-173.html); rule-kind names are from the abstract and not confirmed against the full PDF). The Reality Code Engine's TICK is Dedalus's `@next`; its IN_<source> is `@async` with the nondeterministic arrival pinned by logging. **Bluespec** is closest on δ and O: a module is state plus guarded atomic rules, the compiler chooses a static per-cycle schedule provably equal to one-rule-at-a-time execution, and the design cost figures are 790 lines of TRSPEC versus 930 of Verilog and eight hours versus five days for a MIPS subset ([Hoe & Arvind 2004](https://www.cs.ox.ac.uk/people/bernard.sufrin/personal/HANDEL/Operation-centric_hardware_description_and_synthesis.pdf)). The current spec chose Bluespec's Theorem 1 regime: every rule reads the entry state, so all rules are trivially conflict-free and the post-state is order-independent (spec/book/15, D.5), which forfeits the sequentially-composable EHR regime where a later rule sees an earlier rule's write within the tick ([MIT Memo 492](https://csg.csail.mit.edu/pubs/memos/Memo-492/Memo492.pdf)); the price is that any intra-tick dependency needs two events. **The Elm architecture** is the identity on (S, E, δ, λ): `Model` = S, `Msg` = E, `update : Msg → Model → (Model, Cmd Msg)` = (δ, λ), and the runtime owns D as a time-travel debugger ([Elm Architecture guide](https://guide.elm-lang.org/architecture/)); Elm 0.19 refused user kernel code, the strongest trust coordinate fixed by refusal in the atlas, and has had no release since October 2019 ([Elm 0.19 announcement](https://elm-lang.org/news/small-assets-without-the-headache)). Its loss is that `Cmd`/`Sub` are opaque, so the alphabet is open at the runtime. **Lustre/SCADE** is closest on R and certification: bounded memory "provided that imported functions do", causal models compile to statically scheduled sequential code, KCG is DO-178B qualified and used in "about hundred DO178B/C level A avionic systems" ([Colaço, Pagano, Pouzet 2017](https://www.di.ens.fr/~pouzet/bib/tase17.pdf)); the Form's invariants (every program finishes, loops have limits, one region freed per step) are SCADE's in kind, and the differences in degree — bounded recursion, per-step arenas, maps and text as values — are each a place where certification needs a bound proof rather than a syntactic exclusion. **Unison** is closest on identity: SHA3-512 of the syntax tree, names as metadata, dependencies by hash, typecheck once and cache forever ([Unison](https://www.unison-lang.org/docs/the-big-idea/)), which is the same move as the name-free content-hash order of FORM.md law 2; Dhall shows the price, since a canonical form puts the normaliser in the TCB and "finite does not mean fast" ([Dhall](https://docs.dhall-lang.org/discussions/Safety-guarantees.html)). No chart is close on T or I simultaneously, and none has a hash-chained D as the primary artefact; Smalltalk's `.changes` file got the mechanism right (append-only, replayable after a crash) but logged source edits rather than behaviour, with no prior state and no sync guarantee with the image ([Dias, Cassou, Ducasse 2013](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1309.4334)).

## Hard constraints the atlas imposes on the Form

**Spawn must be stackless.** The ALGOL family has two concurrency ancestries that lower differently. Stackful coroutines (Modula-2 `TRANSFER`, Go goroutines, Cforall, D fibers, Lua coroutines) lower to a runtime with stack switching and cannot become a first-order machine without CPS; stackless constructs (C++20 coroutines, Rust `async`, Swift `async`, Zig 0.10 async, Nim's macro async, Protothreads) are defunctionalized state machines by construction, with the suspended frame as S and the suspension points as control states ([RFC 2394](https://rust-lang.github.io/rfcs/2394-async_await.html)). The Form's δ is on the stackless side by definition, so SURFACE.md's `spawn` must be a region with a mailbox, never a stack, or D cannot capture every resumption. Rust's RFC rejected green threads and stackful coroutines for the same reason, and the cost it accepted — `Pin` — is the warning: the Form IR, not the surface, must own regions and mailboxes, or the author pays the lowering's coordinates. Ada Ravenscar adds the second half of the constraint: the set of regions should be static and known at admission, which is what made Ravenscar statically schedulable and certifiable [bg; Ravenscar not re-fetched].

**`par` must be emitted as a structured step, never an explicit product automaton.** Esterel's three automaton compilers were fast but "none of these techniques are able to compile concurrent programs longer than about 1000 lines" because the product of parallel branches explodes exponentially; the circuit encoding keeps size linear and loses the explicit state graph, at up to 100× slower code, with Edwards' compiler recovering about half the automaton speed ([Benveniste et al. 2003](https://www.cs.columbia.edu/~sedwards/papers/benveniste2003synchronous.pdf)). Any "emit to FSM" target that expands `par` into states will hit the same wall; the normative power-of-two reduction tree and "as if in source order" semantics (spec/book/14, C.8) are the circuit-side choice and should stay that way. This also bounds the Nerode-index measurement below: the minimal machine of a Form with k parallel regions has up to the product of the regions' state counts, so the index of a concurrent Form is a property of the product, not of the source.

**Effects must be one-shot, and the exclusion must be argued from ownership, not loggability.** The spec's F.2 excludes effect handlers because they break T2 (bound computed from the call graph) and T3 (the log would need the continuation). The atlas says T3 is answerable: after evidence passing ([Xie & Leijen, ICFP 2020](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/effect-handlers-evidently/)) or CPS plus Reynolds defunctionalization, a continuation is first-order data, a multi-shot handler is a deterministic fold over a tree of resumptions, and the log needs only external events plus program-determined branch choices. What is not answerable is T2 and ownership: each extra resume duplicates the captured state, aliasing S under another name, and the number of resumes is data-dependent, so no call-graph bound exists. These are exactly the engineering grounds OCaml 5 gave — "resuming the continuation more than once raises an Invalid_argument exception", because "copying fibers is unnecessary" and the primary use is concurrency ([Sivaramakrishnan et al., PLDI 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2104.00250)) — and WasmFX made the same choice ([Phipps-Costin et al., OOPSLA 2023](https://doi.org/10.1145/3622814)). The corollary is a fragment the Form can admit without breaking either theorem: one-shot, tail-resumptive handlers, Koka's fast path, which run in place with no context capture and are observationally a first-order machine already.

**The Nerode index is the only theorem-grade dimension.** The minimal automaton is unique up to isomorphism with state count equal to the index of the Nerode right-congruence ([Nerode 1958](https://doi.org/10.2307/2033204)); for deterministic systems bisimilarity coincides with trace equivalence ([Park 1981](https://doi.org/10.1007/BFb0017309)); and the minimal Mealy machine is synthesised coalgebraically by derivatives ([Rutten, ENTCS 2006](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.entcs.2006.06.006)). For finite S, Definition 3 therefore yields a canonical representative per e-class independent of any cost, which is a stronger property than least-joule extraction. For S containing u64 or text the equivalence is undecidable in general even though every program terminates, so the e-graph under-approximates the orbit, exactly as egg's bounded saturation does. The earlier design document reached the same boundary from the automata side — a unique minimal symbolic finite automaton for Σc with Σd abstracted to guard predicates ([D'Antoni & Veanes, POPL 2014](https://microsoft.com/en-us/research/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/sfaMinimizationPOPL14.pdf)) — and the atlas confirms it from the coalgebraic side: the dimension of a Form is the Nerode index of its Σc-plus-predicates projection, and nothing else about its size is a theorem.

**The lift is partial with an undecidable domain.** Three independent results converge. The Form requires termination (every program finishes), so the lift from any Turing-complete source is at best a map from the terminating fragment, and membership in that fragment is undecidable. Gurevich's theorem covers sequential bounded-exploration algorithms and the parallel case is contested. And even on the terminating fragment the lift is not canonical: closure representation, which sub-terms become control states, and evaluation order (λv versus λn CPS, [Plotkin 1975](https://doi.org/10.1016/0304-3975(75)90017-1)) are free choices, and the inverse exists only for Forms in defunctionalized form ([Danvy & Millikin 2008](https://www.brics.dk/RS/08/4/)). The correct statement of U3 is that the Form is a global chart on the sub-space of total, bounded-exploration, sequential behaviours; the lift from higher-order charts into that sub-space is a theorem (Reynolds/Danvy for control, game semantics for typed functional cores, since the game-semantic interpretation and the abstract-machine execution are the same object per Danos–Herbelin–Regnier), and outside it the Form is a partial chart. This changes the earlier design document's position on λ_L from "a construction on the image" to "a construction on the image of a partial map whose domain is undecidable", which is a sharper refusal, not a retreat.

Two softer constraints come from the logic and hardware rows. The Reality Code Engine's derived `monotone` tag ("no resolve, no read, no join, no scan") is a sound, incomplete syntactic approximation of CALM's semantic property; Bloom classifies by operator (join is monotone, anti-join and set minus are not), so refusing all joins is strictly more conservative than the literature, and adopting the finer classification requires proving relations insert-only, which the spec itself flags as missing ([Keeping CALM](https://ar5iv.labs.arxiv.org/html/1901.01930); spec/book/15 D.5). DBSP then supplies the algebra the earlier design document's "log is the database" needs: S_t = I(D)[t], indexes are I∘Q evaluated non-incrementally today, and the chain rule Q^Δ = D∘Q∘I makes them incremental mechanically, with a Lean artefact to port and a Rust implementation that passes all 7 million SQL Logic Tests ([Budiu et al., VLDB 2023](https://www.vldb.org/pvldb/vol16/p1601-budiu.pdf)). From the HDL row: a cost report is trustworthy only because the lowering is deterministic and the source cannot allocate; the Form's "one region freed per step" and normative reduction tree are what make a synthesis-style report possible, and the remaining gap is a per-fabric joule table that HDL tools get from switching activity and the Form must get from measurement, which is the regime-A/regime-B calibration the earlier document specified.

## What the Form is, coalgebraically, and what "dimension of code" means

Stated without the string-theory vocabulary: a Form is an F-coalgebra ⟨S, δ⟩ for F(X) = (O* × X)^E, with D the finite prefix of the input stream and λ the output projection; its behaviour is the unique coalgebra morphism into the final coalgebra, and two Forms are equivalent (Definition 3) iff their behaviour maps agree, which is Mealy equivalence, which is final-coalgebra equality. Totality means δ is a total function rather than a relation, so the behaviour map is total on every input word; the hash-chained D is a particular finite element of E* with an integrity proof attached; the per-step arena and LOOP_LIMIT bound the state space so that the behaviour map is finitely presented. The space of behaviours is the final coalgebra with the prefix ultrametric: complete, totally disconnected, zero-dimensional, equipped with a universal property rather than smoothness. Languages are functors from their initial syntax algebras into that final coalgebra, many-to-one, with domains that differ by macro-expressibility; translations are natural transformations between those functors that are invertible only in the named cases; the e-graph is the fundamental groupoid of the rewrite relation restricted to one behaviour; extraction is MDL under a cost table; binding time is a decidable gauge direction on representations.

"Dimension of code" then has exactly one theorem-grade meaning and two measurable-but-not-intrinsic proxies. The theorem-grade meaning is the Nerode index of the minimal machine of the Form's finite-control projection: the number of distinguishable states, language-independent, canonical, and computable in n log n for an explicit alphabet or in O(n² log n · f(nℓ)) solver calls for the symbolic alphabet of guard predicates. The first proxy is description length — cross-entropy under a language model or MDL code length under a model class — which is what the naturalness literature measures and which is corpus-dependent by construction (Hindle, Rahman, Casalnuovo). The second proxy is an embedding manifold's intrinsic dimension, which has been measured for natural-language token embeddings and never for code. Neither proxy is a property of the behaviour; both are properties of a representation under a model. The honest statement for the paper is that a Form has an intrinsic dimension (its Nerode index on Σc), that its data component Σd has no theorem-grade dimension (the only canonical object for arithmetic state is the term itself, as the earlier design document established), and that any entropy or embedding number is a measurement of the chart, not the point.

## Open problems and the measurements nobody has done

The first missing measurement is the **Nerode index of real corpora**. No corpus study of minimal-state-machine sizes of real programs exists; the symbolic-automata and GKAT machinery to compute it on the finite-control projection exists, so the experiment is a tool run, not a theory problem: minimise the Σc-plus-guards projection of every Form in the stdlib and the L0–L7 benchmark sets, report the index distribution, and compare it with the term size and the e-class count. The number would be the first intrinsic-dimension figure for code that is not corpus-relative, and it would settle whether the "code is low-entropy" literature is measuring a small behaviour space or a redundant chart.

The second is the **quantitative holonomy of a translation pair**. Every transpiler loss in the atlas is structural (which constructs have no image: c2rust emits `unsafe` with raw pointers and ownership is unrecoverable; wasm2c re-emits linear memory as a byte array; TypeScript erasure drops all types on the forward map) and none is measured. The experiment is a corpus translated C → Rust → C or Python → Form → Python with a behavioural diff at each cycle, reported as the fraction of the e-class fixed by the cycle and the code-size growth of defunctionalization, which no source quantifies either. The one number that exists, Typed Racket's 88–104× at boundaries, measures the holonomy of a type re-expansion loop, not of a translation loop. The Reality Code Engine is unusually well placed to run this because `rce emit` plus `rce test` already manufactures translation pairs with an oracle, which the earlier design document adopted as the MultiPL-T recipe in reverse ([MultiPL-T, OOPSLA 2024](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2308.09895)).

Beyond those two, the atlas leaves the following open, in decreasing order of how much a result would change the Form. Whether refunctionalization composed with direct-style transformation gives a total inverse of the full Reynolds pipeline on arbitrary first-order machines is not published; the Danvy-school results are stated on evaluators and specific programs, and a positive answer would make FSM-out canonical. No paper proves equivalence between an effect-handler program and an event-logged replay in the Form's sense; the one-shot-fragment admission above rests on a synthesis. The parallel ASM thesis is contested, so the Form's claim to cover parallel algorithms has no theorem behind it yet. No Riemannian or Finsler metric on program or behaviour space exists in the literature, and the ultrametric and quantale-enriched settings are the only metric structures; a physicist expecting to find a connection will not. Dedalus's rule-kind semantics were read from the abstract only; Mercury's determinism categories, Verse's confluence theorem, Ameloot's exact JACM statement, Erlang's pairwise-FIFO text and Go's random `select` clause are all cited from background and need a citation pass. No source gives Bluespec or any HDL energy per operation versus hand Verilog, only area and time, so the "HDL world knows joules" claim is true of the flow and unproved of Bluespec specifically. No one has measured LLM token counts for APL/J/K against Python, so "one glyph per primitive" cannot be claimed as token efficiency; the glyphs are multi-byte and rare in tokenizers. The per-language token-cost table of the 2026 tokenmaxxing study was not retrievable ([Wu, Anderson, Guha 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.22807), abstract only). And the per-row adoption claims for Zig, Odin and Jai, the Ravenscar profile text, the Protothreads paper, Veldhuizen 2003, the C99 Rationale, Wirth 1995, the Ada 83 Rationale and the HOPL IV JavaScript account are all [not re-fetched] in the lineage notes; the writer has not promoted any of them to a quantitative claim here.

## Conclusion

The reconciliation leaves the author's intuition intact and its vocabulary replaced. Every structure the string-theory frame reached for exists, but in coalgebra, category theory and information theory rather than differential geometry: the universal property of the final coalgebra does the work the manifold was supposed to do, the rewrite groupoid does what the gauge group was supposed to do, and MDL does what the geodesic was supposed to do. The one genuinely geometric result in the field — Danos, Herbelin and Regnier's identification of game-semantic strategies with abstract-machine execution — points the same direction as the atlas: higher-order programs become first-order token-passing automata, which is the Form's shape. What the atlas adds that no single theorem does is the record of what sixty years of charts integrated out and what it cost them: the log, intent and energy coordinates were compactified by every family, and the Form's claim to fix all eight at once is defensible precisely because no predecessor tried.

The practical consequence is that the Form's remaining risk is not in its theorems but in the five constraints above, each of which is a place where a predecessor paid for the opposite choice: Go paid for stackful spawn with an unloggable scheduler, Esterel paid for explicit automata with a thousand-line ceiling, Eff paid for multi-shot with aliased state, UNCOL paid for a total lift with non-existence, and every entropy study paid for a corpus-relative dimension with a number that moves when the corpus does. The two measurements that would close the frame — Nerode indices over a real corpus and a holonomy figure for one translation pair — are runnable with the tooling the project already has, and either result would be the first of its kind.
