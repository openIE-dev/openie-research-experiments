**Corrections and reproducibility status (17 October 2026).** This note was added after a read of the paper against the public repositories. The figures it affects are listed here and the text below is otherwise unchanged. Every count below comes from [`src/data/rce-facts.json`](https://github.com/openIE-dev/research-openie-web/blob/dcharlot65-openie/src/data/rce-facts.json), produced by `scripts/claim-counts.py` in [openIE-dev/rce](https://github.com/openIE-dev/rce) at `e416fd0`, except the two Lean counts, which were taken at `4304048`.

- **One language (18 October 2026).** Public `rce` main (`4a3dc1f`) has a single language and a single text. A `# rce-version:` line is a tolerated, ignored release label that is not hashed, and a release label names exactly one specification commit (C.15). A construct the checker does not implement is refused as reserved, not implemented (W-RES-1, Appendix F.12), not by version; there is one reserved word list. Section 3.7 describes this one language. A failed call restores its `&mut` arguments (F.4.4), which replaces the write-back rule of theorem T33. The counts below were taken at `e416fd0`, before this change, and were not rerun. The proof of T33 changed with the rule: the retired write-back-on-every-outcome theorems left the audited tree (public `rce` main `95ead2e`), and T33 now proves the F.4.4 restore; the audit reads 4875 theorems with standard axioms only.
- **Library names (rce-stdlib, 18 October 2026).** Because the reserved list is global, library names that it now reserves were renamed: `label` to `lbl`, `node` to `nd`, `group` to `grp`, `child` to `chd`, `budget` to `bud`, `restart` to `rst`, `replicated` to `repl`, `ct` to `ctext`, `session` to `sess`, `protocol` to `proto`, `par` to `parl`, `offer` to `offr`, `choose` to `chse`. The types `Json` (module `enc`) and `Url` (module `net.url`) are now `JsonDoc` and `UrlParts`, so a program that imports `enc.Json` or `net.url.Url` must use the new names. The text length `.len()` of a `text` value is `.len_bytes()` (bytes) or `.len_chars()`.
- **Counts at `e416fd0`.** The specification document is draft 0.9 (public since `cdae5cf`). Theorems T1 to T56 are stated; T42 to T56 (Appendix F) are stated and unproved. There are 58 Lean modules and 59 example programs (57 top-level and 2 in packages). The figures and captions in this paper that say draft 0.7, T1 to T34, 31 files or 51 examples describe the repository at the earlier commits they name, and this paper does not describe draft 0.9 beyond this note.
- **Lean theorem count.** Two different counts exist. The source holds 1,116 `theorem` and `lemma` declarations at `e416fd0` (885 at `4304048`). The audit (`scripts/lean-audit.sh`) counts every theorem constant in the kernel environment, including constants the elaborator generates, and gave 4,877 on a rerun over the public repository at `e416fd0`, where `lake build` completes over 58 files of `spec/lean/RCE/` with no `sorry` and standard axioms only (2,949 over 46 files at `4304048`). The 2,192 quoted below is the audit count at engine commit `1eb5f02` over 31 modules. Neither number says anything about how many theorems are proved about the Rust evaluator.
- **Reproducible from public repositories today:** the Lean build and axiom audit, the specification check (grammar tables and example corpora), the reference checker and its tests, and the canonical JSON vectors.
- **Not yet reproducible from public repositories:** the 10,000-Form ecosystem run (`bench/ecosystem` is not in the public repositories), the Kani results, the 589 engine tests, the repair rates of Section 6 and any Defects4J number. They are reported here as the authors' own runs and should be read that way until the sources and seeds are published.
- **Falsifier RCE-F0.** See the note in Section 2.3 below.
